About these mockups

These are visual design mockups, not live sites or live social accounts. They demonstrate the proposed look, feel, and content strategy.

How to use the theme switcher:

  • Open either mockup and use the "Choose your look" bar at the top to switch between the 4 logo/color options
  • A floating button in the bottom-right corner lets you switch themes while scrolling
  • The selected theme is saved between page refreshes
  • Share a specific option using the URL — e.g. website.html#theme-2

What's shown:

  • 4 distinct visual themes — one for each logo option — covering colors, accents, and typography
  • Inline booking form (Schedule Service) wired to the FieldPro scheduling backend
  • Sample social post copy demonstrating Gulf Coast HVAC seasonality and brand voice
  • The Progressive Web App home-screen install pattern

What's a placeholder:

  • All numbers (years in business, review count, follower counts, engagement)
  • All testimonial quotes and customer names
  • Phone number (uses 555-prefix reserved for fictional use)
  • Email, license number, and other contact details

Real numbers, real reviews, and real contact details replace every [bracketed] placeholder before production launch.
